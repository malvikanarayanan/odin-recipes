Pad Kra Pow Recipe

Home

Pad Kra Prow is a classic, fiery Thai street food stir-fry featuring minced meat or tofu tossed with pungent garlic, spicy chilies, and aromatic holy basil leaves. It is intensely savory, slightly sweet, and traditionally crowned with a crispy, runny fried egg over steamed jasmine rice.

To make this quick meal, fry a couple of eggs in hot oil until the edges are beautifully crisp, then set them aside. In the same pan, flash-fry minced garlic and bird's eye chilies before adding ground pork, chicken, or beef, breaking it apart on high heat until cooked through. Toss the meat in a quick sauce made of oyster sauce, soy sauce, fish sauce, and sugar, then turn off the flame and fold in fresh holy basil leaves until they wilt into the savory mixture before serving immediately over warm jasmine rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground chicken, pork, or beef (or finely chopped tofu)
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 5 clovesgarlic, minced or pounded in a mortar and pestle
  • 1 cupfresh holy basil leaves

Steps

  1. Heat a generous splash of oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat until it is shimmering and very hot.
  2. Crack an egg directly into the oil. Let it fry without moving it until the edges are crispy, browned, and bubbly, but the yolk is still runny.
  3. Remove the egg, set it aside on a plate, and repeat with the second egg.
